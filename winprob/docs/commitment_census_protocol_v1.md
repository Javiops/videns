2026-09-30. Counts-only specification frozen before this new census. No model,
win target, causal effect or policy is fitted. The failed auxiliary-goal recipe
remains closed. This study changes the decision unit, not its loss weight.

The options framework treats an action as a policy executed over a variable
duration (Sutton, Precup and Singh,1999,
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0004370299000521).
Our prospective adaptation would value a build option o through
Q(s,o)=E[sum_{k=0}^{tau-1} gamma^k r_{t+k+1}+gamma^tau V(s_{t+tau})|s,o].
This equation supplies a possible future estimand, not identification from our
observational archive. Today we only measure whether recorded initiation and
completion states can be distinguished with adequate support. Neither an
observed future item nor a purchased generic component proves private intent.

cases and70 cases with the separate ordinary-Boots credit sensitivity. These
are conservative support counts, not the total of actually legal choices.
The new population differs; compare initiation/completion ONLY within this
new4001-game population, never divide by the old19 to claim a gain.
